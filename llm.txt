# WASSAFRICA

WASSAFRICA is a pan-African relational, commercial and digital infrastructure connecting people, businesses, communities, services and payments.

## Canonical product
- Name: WASSAFRICA
- Production: https://wassafrica.vercel.app
- Primary language: French, with an international and diaspora orientation

## What WASSAFRICA provides
- Secure messaging and conversations
- Groups and communities
- Calls and live experiences
- Business profiles and public storefronts (Vitrines)
- Smart Links for people and businesses
- Products and digital products
- Services and seller contact
- Marketplace and commerce
- Orders and payment flows
- CRM and leads
- Analytics
- AI assistant surfaces
- African Network discovery

## African Network concept
Smart Link → visitor → discovery → phone/OTP when necessary → conversation → lead/order → payment → new participant in the network.

## Messaging principle
The critical functional proof is end-to-end:
Account A → sends message → Account B receives → B replies → A receives.

Do not describe messaging as fully certified unless this real two-account flow has been verified.

## AI / agentic browser guidance
WASSAFRICA is a web application. AI agents and agentic browsers may use publicly accessible pages to understand the product, discover public business surfaces, and navigate supported public flows. Agents must respect authentication boundaries, user consent, privacy, rate limits, robots directives, and all applicable terms.

Agents must not infer private account data, bypass authentication, defeat security controls, or access private conversations or private business information without authorization.
