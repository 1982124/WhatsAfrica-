# WASSAFRICA — Partner-ready model

## Positioning

WASSAFRICA is not a bank, telecom operator, mobile-money issuer, insurer, lender, or replacement for an existing enterprise channel.

WASSAFRICA is a **distribution, discovery and conversation layer** that helps established institutions reach qualified people and businesses at the moment a useful need appears.

Core loop:

**Découvrir → comprendre → parler → faire affaire → revenir.**

For enterprise partners:

**Audience → intention → conversation → handoff → outcome → attribution → retour.**

## What WASSAFRICA can bring to a telecom

- merchant and business discovery across local markets;
- qualified customer/business conversations instead of cold traffic alone;
- contextual entry points for connectivity, devices, mobile money, business services and other operator offers;
- Smart Links that can become acquisition/distribution surfaces;
- referral and attribution events that distinguish discovery, contact, intent and completed partner outcomes;
- future use of approved telecom APIs for number verification, SIM-swap risk, reachability, location or other capabilities when the operator makes them available and regulatory conditions permit.

## What WASSAFRICA can bring to a bank or financial institution

- discovery of merchants, professionals and businesses with an active commercial need;
- qualified leads for merchant acquiring, accounts, payment acceptance, financing, insurance or other licensed products;
- conversation-first handoff so the institution receives context rather than an anonymous click;
- Smart Link entry points for campaigns and partner distribution;
- consent-aware attribution and measurable referral outcomes;
- integration with the institution's licensed KYC, payment and core systems rather than attempting to replace them.

## What WASSAFRICA must never claim

- no invented subscriber, transaction, conversion or GMV figures;
- no claim of bank, telecom, payment, lending, insurance or KYC licensing unless independently established;
- no promise of regulated financial outcomes before partner approval;
- no sale or sharing of personal data outside an explicit lawful basis and agreed partner scope;
- no fabricated enterprise logos, certifications, SLAs or production integrations.

## Partnership architecture

### 1. Discovery

WASSAFRICA exposes a relevant business, service or opportunity through a Smart Link, marketplace surface, campaign or direct referral.

### 2. Qualification

The platform records only the minimum useful event needed to understand intent: for example `view`, `contact_started`, `call_started`, `negotiation_started`, `referral_clicked` or a partner-defined outcome.

### 3. Conversation

The user can speak with the business/person first. Where a partner offer is relevant, the user can voluntarily enter the partner journey without losing context.

### 4. Handoff

A partner receives a structured referral/handoff according to an agreed API or secure export contract. WASSAFRICA does not impersonate the partner or bypass its compliance process.

### 5. Outcome

The partner can return a coarse-grained outcome such as `accepted`, `qualified`, `converted`, `rejected` or `pending`, subject to the commercial and privacy agreement.

### 6. Attribution

Both parties can reconcile referral IDs and timestamps so the commercial value of WASSAFRICA is measurable.

## Minimum enterprise trust package

Before a production partnership, WASSAFRICA should be able to present:

- legal entity and contracting information;
- privacy and data-processing documentation;
- security architecture and access-control model;
- API documentation and sandbox process;
- referral/attribution specification;
- incident and support process;
- pilot scope with measurable acceptance criteria;
- data retention/deletion rules;
- clear responsibilities between WASSAFRICA and the regulated partner.

## Pilot model

Start narrow rather than promising continent-wide scale immediately.

**Pilot = 1 country + 1 partner business line + 1 measurable customer journey + 1 attribution contract.**

Example telecom pilot:

**Smart Link → merchant discovery → interest in business connectivity → partner handoff → activation outcome.**

Example bank pilot:

**Smart Link → merchant discovery → interest in payment acceptance → partner handoff → onboarding outcome.**

The pilot should measure:

- unique referred prospects;
- qualified referrals;
- conversation/contact rate;
- partner handoff rate;
- accepted applications;
- completed outcomes;
- time to outcome;
- attributable commercial value;
- complaint/fraud/abuse rate.

## Commercial principle

WASSAFRICA should be paid for **measurable distribution value**, not for pretending to be the institution itself.

Possible structures, to be negotiated per partner and regulation:

- qualified lead fee;
- successful referral fee;
- activation/acquisition fee;
- revenue share where lawful and commercially appropriate;
- campaign/distribution fee;
- enterprise API/platform fee.

No commercial model is hard-coded into the product before a signed agreement.

## Strategic advantage

The strongest pitch is not:

> "WASSAFRICA remplace votre banque ou votre opérateur."

It is:

> **"WASSAFRICA vous apporte des personnes et des entreprises déjà dans une démarche réelle, au moment où votre offre peut résoudre leur besoin."**

That makes WASSAFRICA a potential **distribution partner**, not a threat to the incumbent's core business.
