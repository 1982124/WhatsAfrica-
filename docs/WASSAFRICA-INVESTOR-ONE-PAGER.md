# WASSAFRICA — Investor One-Pager

## The thesis

> **WASSAFRICA rend les affaires africaines plus faciles à découvrir, comprendre, démarrer et conclure.**

## What is WASSAFRICA?

WASSAFRICA is building a mobile-first African experience layer connecting discovery, conversation, commerce and access to partner services.

The core journey is:

**VOIR → COMPRENDRE → PARLER → FAIRE AFFAIRE → REVENIR**

## The problem

Users and businesses frequently navigate fragmented journeys across search, social profiles, phone calls, messaging, payments and service-provider applications.

The opportunity is to reduce the number of handoffs between intent and outcome.

## The product

### Smart Link

A shareable business/service representation that can bring together:

- what the activity offers;
- where it is;
- how to contact it;
- conversation;
- useful actions;
- voluntary recommendation.

### Conversation

Discovery should naturally lead to a real conversation rather than a dead-end listing.

### Commerce

Where the underlying capabilities are proven, users can move from conversation to commercial action.

### Partner Network

WASSAFRICA can act as a distribution/handoff layer for services provided by regulated or specialized partners.

**WASSAFRICA hosts the experience and relationship; the partner provides the regulated service.**

## Why this can scale

The potential network connects:

**Users → businesses → conversations → services → partners → outcomes → recommendations**

Each useful relationship can create additional discovery and distribution.

## Business model

Potential revenue streams include referral, acquisition, activation, distribution, transaction-linked economics where legally applicable, and selected B2B SaaS/API services.

No revenue figure is presented here unless backed by documented accounts.

## Current stage

**Closure / proof phase.**

The operating sequence is:

**PROVE → DEPLOY → CERTIFY → NEGOTIATE → PILOT → SCALE**

Production certification and partner/live claims require evidence and are not assumed from code or UI presence.

## What funding unlocks

- production hardening;
- measurable user journeys;
- partner pilots;
- security and compliance;
- integration infrastructure;
- commercial execution;
- country-by-country expansion after proof.

## The long-term ambition

Build a trusted African distribution and relationship layer that becomes a starting point for real economic activity.

> **Not more features. Less friction between African intent and African outcomes.**
