# WASSAFRICA — Partner Target Map 2026

This is a target list, not a partnership list. Every entry starts as `target / outreach`, never `partner`, until there is written confirmation or a signed pilot.

## Priority A — distribution + payments

### MTN MoMo
Why target:
- African mobile-money footprint.
- Public APIs cover collections, disbursements and remittances.
- Relevant to merchant acquisition, payments and diaspora journeys.

First ask:
- One-country pilot.
- One payment/remittance journey.
- Referral or approved API/handoff.
- Attribution and contractual commission.

Official starting points:
- https://momo.mtn.com/api/
- https://momodeveloper.mtn.com/

### Orange / Orange Developer
Why target:
- Public API catalog covering payments, identity/fraud and network capabilities.
- Strong fit for a Smart Link → service-access model.

First ask:
- Country-specific API availability.
- Orange Money Web Payment or another approved service.
- One-country pilot with attribution.

Official starting point:
- https://developer.orange.com/

### Ecobank
Why target:
- Pan-African banking API proposition.
- Public developer portal includes account opening, collection, payments and remittance services.

First ask:
- Merchant/business acquisition or account-opening journey.
- One country and one business segment.
- Referral/handoff first; API after pilot validation.

Official starting point:
- https://apimuat-developer.ecobank.com/

### Flutterwave
Why target:
- Broad payment APIs and payment links.
- Relevant to commerce, merchant payments and cross-border payment experiences.

First ask:
- Country-specific availability.
- Payment-link or checkout pilot.
- Attribution and commercial model.

Official starting point:
- https://developer.flutterwave.com/

## Priority B — network API / telecom ecosystem

### COURE
Why target:
- GSMA Open Gateway channel partner focused on secure telecom APIs, KYC, fraud prevention and routing in Africa.
- Potential fit for WASSAFRICA's future trust and network-service layer.

First ask:
- One fraud-prevention or number-verification use case.
- One country.
- Sandbox or controlled pilot.

Important:
- Do not claim GSMA partnership for WASSAFRICA.
- Do not imply access to operator APIs until contract and technical onboarding are completed.

### NetAPI Telecom
Why target:
- African GSMA Open Gateway channel partner and API aggregator.
- Potential route to standardized network capabilities across operators and countries.

First ask:
- Number verification / SIM Swap / device intelligence use case.
- One country and one enterprise journey.
- Commercial + technical pilot.

Important:
- API availability and regulatory permission remain country-specific.

## Priority C — vertical services

### Insurance partner
Target products:
- vehicle
- travel
- professional
- household
- relevant micro-insurance

Pilot principle:
WASSAFRICA distributes and contextualizes; insurer owns product, underwriting, KYC, contract and claims.

### Logistics partner
Target products:
- merchant delivery
- last-mile
- pickup
- parcel tracking

Pilot principle:
WASSAFRICA provides demand and context; logistics provider owns operations, pricing and delivery status.

### Diaspora/remittance partner
Target:
- one corridor first
- one use case first
- one regulated provider first

Pilot principle:
WASSAFRICA is the discovery and access layer; the regulated partner executes the financial service.

## Strategic sequencing

1. MTN / Orange / one bank or PSP
2. One measurable payment or merchant-acquisition pilot
3. One network-API trust/fraud pilot
4. One diaspora corridor
5. One insurance or logistics service
6. Expand only after real outcomes

## Truth-state vocabulary

- `TARGET` — identified, no outreach yet.
- `OUTREACH_READY` — message prepared.
- `CONTACTED` — message actually sent.
- `DISCOVERY` — meeting scheduled or held.
- `PILOT_NEGOTIATION` — scope/commercial/compliance being negotiated.
- `PILOT_APPROVED` — written confirmation exists.
- `INTEGRATING` — technical onboarding is underway.
- `LIVE_PILOT` — real users can use the service under agreed scope.
- `CONVERTED` — agreed success criteria met.
- `SCALED` — expanded beyond the initial pilot.

No UI, document, investor deck or public page may use `partner`, `integrated`, `live`, `certified` or `commissioning` for a target unless the corresponding truth-state evidence exists.
